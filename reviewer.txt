PHOTOSYNTHESIS

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make their own food using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water. It mainly occurs in the chloroplasts of plant cells.

CELL

The cell is the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells. Cells contain structures that perform different functions.

MITOSIS

Mitosis is a process of cell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells. It is important for growth, development, and tissue repair.

DNA

DNA is a molecule that carries genetic information in living organisms. It contains instructions that help determine the characteristics and functions of an organism.

RESPIRATION

Cellular respiration is the process by which cells release energy from food. This energy is used by cells to perform different activities.

ECOSYSTEM

An ecosystem is a community of living organisms interacting with one another and with their physical environment. It includes both biotic and abiotic components.

FOOD CHAIN

A food chain shows how energy and nutrients are transferred from one organism to another. It usually begins with producers and continues through consumers.

PRODUCERS

Producers are organisms that can make their own food. Plants are common producers because they use photosynthesis to produce food.

CONSUMERS

Consumers are organisms that cannot make their own food and must obtain energy by eating other organisms. Animals are examples of consumers.

GRAVITY

Gravity is a force that attracts objects toward one another. On Earth, gravity pulls objects toward the center of the planet.

FORCE

A force is a push or pull that can change the motion of an object. Force can cause an object to start moving, stop, speed up, slow down, or change direction.

MATTER

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space. The three common states of matter are solid, liquid, and gas.

ATOM

An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains the chemical properties of that element. Atoms are made up of protons, neutrons, and electrons.

ELEMENT

An element is a pure substance made up of only one type of atom. Examples include oxygen, hydrogen, carbon, and gold.

WATER CYCLE

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water through the environment. It includes processes such as evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.
